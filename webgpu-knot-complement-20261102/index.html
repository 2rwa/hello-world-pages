Construction

The (2,3) torus knot is K(u)=((R+r cos 3u)cos 2u,(R+r cos 3u)sin 2u,r sin 3u). We remove a radius-ρ neighborhood of a 48-edge polygonal approximation. The remaining space is the complement; its dark boundary is topologically a torus. Red meridian markings indicate the local circle around the excluded core.

Exact point-to-segment distance is minimized over all polygon edges. A torus-surface lower bound with 0.07 polygon-chord safety margin skips empty space; near the knot, conservative distance steps approach the excluded boundary. The guide stays in free space.
